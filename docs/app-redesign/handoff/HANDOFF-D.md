## Decisions recorded (round-1 review)

- **Classic hero and `HeroUnderBars`:** closed, deliberately not called. The classic view draws an opaque `ReportTopBar` on `surface` above the `HeroBand`, as the renderer does (renderer-*-01-hero: paper bar over the dark hero). Running the hero under the bar would need an over-hero palette for `ReportTopBar` (a kit change) and would differ from the page it copies. The sample does call `HeroUnderBars()` (it sits under Today's transparent root bar).
- **Sample without `ChapterPills`:** deliberate. The sample sits under Today's root bar with the hero running under it; a sticky pill row would stick under that bar, and the sample has three or four short chapters. Revisit if the sample gets its own `ReportTopBar`.
- **One Pro upsell per page:** a free user's classic page shows no what-if block; the desk's conviction section carries the single Callout, whose copy then names the what-if model too (`DeepDiveCopy.deskPro`). The new view's tool dialog keeps each tool's own upsell (`DeepDiveConvictionSection(upsell = …)` defaults to the old copy).
- **Sample company name:** the asset has no issuer name; `sampleWithIssuer` shows "Microsoft" (ticker in the meta line) only for the MSFT sample, and the sample note names the company.
- **Five-year series with gaps:** years with no value are dropped (the sample: FY2020, FY2022, FY2024, FY2025), the subtitle says "selected fiscal years", and margins over selected years are columns, not a line across uneven gaps.

- **Basis segments:** "Fiscal year", "Year ago", "Last quarter" (three segments fit at 360 dp; "On the quarter" was cut).
- **Harness note (WP-K / harness owner):** a 360 dp render of the classic page tall enough to show the end of the list deadlocks Compose Desktop: the scroll-spy selects the last chapter and `ChapterPills` scrolls its row with `animateScrollBy` inside a frame callback (README "deadlock"). `dd-classic-whole-360` therefore stops at 4,500 dp. On a device this is harmless, but `ChapterPills` could jump instead of animate when the scroll-spy changes during a render.

## Decisions recorded (round-2 review)

- **Evidence chapter with explainers only:** titled "Reading notes" / "How to read the numbers" (`ClassicChapter.Reading`), so the kicker never promises sources the chapter does not have (the sample). Unsupported claims, missing-data notes, the sparse-data note and trust scorecards now count as evidence too (they were drawn but did not open the chapter).
- **Visual panels:** every type has one home. Trust scorecards are drawn in Evidence ("Report checks", DataRows); range bands are drawn in Price and value, except one that repeats the valuation's own range to the cent; the other types were already drawn by their chapter. A non-metric panel that sits in `financialPanels` only because its title says "margin" is drawn by its own chapter.
- **Range chart title:** generated from where the price in report sits (outside the range, against the base case in whole percent, or inside); "Bear to bull model range" is the subtitle's first sentence.
- **What-if result:** rows use `ClassicFormat.rangeMoney`, the range bar's own formatter ("$228", "$241.50").
- **Indicator groups:** a period shared by every row is said once in the kicker ("Profitability, FY2025"); rows name a period only when the group mixes periods.
- **Coherence period:** ISO dates are parsed; a whole month reads "October 2025", other ranges use `Fmt.weekRange`; shown as a sans caption under the card's title, not in a kicker.
- **Follow toggle (items 1 and 9):** unchanged; still a merge blocker for WP-INT. Export progress (item 5), kill-switch readings (item 6), metric units (item 10) and the sample range-bar waiver (item 11) stay open as before.
