# Legal drafts: questions for counsel

**DRAFT - requires legal review before publication**

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service drafts live in one place:
`apps/product-api/src/legal/legal-texts.ts`. product-api serves them at
`/legal/privacy` and `/legal/terms`. The app links to them at sign-up/login and in the You tab.

Fill every `[PLACEHOLDER]` and resolve every `[VERIFY: ...]` note in that file before publication.
The launch market (US or EU) is not decided, so both drafts have a US part and an EEA/UK part.

## Questions

1. **Signal wording.** The app shows automated signals on named stocks, for example "Positive 7/10".
   Is this an "investment recommendation" under the EU Market Abuse Regulation (MAR, Art. 3(1)(35))
   and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/958? If yes, what disclosures (identity, methodology, date, conflicts)
   must appear next to each signal? Would different wording (for example "evidence balance") change the answer?
2. **Investment advice / authorisation (EU and UK).** Could the product be "investment advice" under
   MiFID II, or a regulated activity under the UK FSMA / FCA rules, given that users enter watchlists and
   holdings and receive alerts on those names? Is a personalisation boundary needed?
3. **US adviser status.** Can we rely on the publisher exclusion of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940
   (Lowe v. SEC: impersonal, bona fide, regular publication)? Do holdings-linked alerts or
   "kill switch" rules put this at risk? Any state-level issues?
4. **Disclaimer sufficiency.** Is the "research tool, not financial advice" notice in onboarding, login,
   reports and the You tab enough, or must it appear on each signal or each alert notification?
5. **AI-generated content.** What disclosure is required for LLM-written text (EU AI Act transparency
   rules, US FTC guidance)? Is the wording in Terms Section 3 enough?
6. **Analytics consent.** The app sends product analytics (PostHog) without an opt-in today. In the EEA/UK,
   is prior consent required (ePrivacy Art. 5(3) / PECR)? If yes, we add an in-app toggle before launch.
7. **LLM providers and transfers.** Report text is generated by US LLM providers (Anthropic, OpenAI,
   OpenRouter, depending on configuration). Prompts mainly hold public data and tickers the user follows.
   Is a DPA plus SCCs/DPF enough? Is a list of tickers a user follows personal data that reveals
   financial information needing extra care?
8. **Retention periods.** What periods should we set for audit logs, server logs, analytics and backups?
   There are no automatic purge jobs yet.
9. **Android backup.** The app allows Android backup (allowBackup=true). Is that acceptable, or should
   we exclude account tokens and research data?
10. **Consumer law (EU).** Wording of the 14-day withdrawal right and the consent step for Google Play
    subscriptions; whether the liability cap and governing-law clauses are enforceable against consumers.
11. **US disputes.** Do we want an arbitration clause and class action waiver? Which governing law and venue?
12. **Minimum age and eligible countries.** 18+? Which countries do we list at launch?
13. **CCPA/CPRA and other state laws.** Do we meet the thresholds? If not, how much of Part B should stay?
14. **EU/UK representative and DPO.** Needed if the company has no EU/UK establishment (GDPR Art. 27)?
